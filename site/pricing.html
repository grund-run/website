Questions people ask.

What counts as a machine?
Any computer grund runs your apps on: a mini PC, a server you own, or one you rent. The laptop you deploy from doesn't count.
Can I run all of it myself?
Yes, always. grund is open source first: every part, including the dashboard, runs self-hosted on your own machines. Our hosted services are a convenience, never a requirement.
Do my apps need a domain?
No. Every app gets an address on grund's domain, even on a home connection without open ports. On Pro and Business you can use your own domain, or hand it to grund and it creates the addresses for you.
Is the free machine a trial?
No. Self-hosted is free and open source, and your first machine on Homelab is free for as long as you use it.
Why is there no license on grund machines?
Because the rental price already covers it. A grund machine costs about what the same server would cost you elsewhere plus a Pro license.
Do you charge for traffic, apps or storage?
No. Only machines are priced. Your hardware or provider covers the rest, and grund machines include it.
What happens to my apps if I stop paying?
On your own machines they keep running, and you drop back to the free machine. A grund machine is a rental, so it ends with your plan; your data can be exported first.
When can I buy it?
grund is in development. These prices are our plan and may change before launch. Follow the build on GitHub to hear first.
